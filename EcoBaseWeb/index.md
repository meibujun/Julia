# Spatio-temporal Digital Foundation
## Data Analysis & Processing

Welcome to the official website of the Spatio-temporal Digital Foundation. We focus on applying mathematical models and computer science technologies to solve complex ecological problems.

### Our Mission

* **Mathematical Modeling:** Utilizing advanced statistical and deterministic models to understand ecological dynamics.
* **Data Processing:** High-performance computing solutions for large-scale spatio-temporal data.
* **Open Source:** Commitment to open science by publishing our code and tools.
